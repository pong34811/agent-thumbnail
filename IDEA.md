# agent-thumbnail

An AI agent that automatically creates, tests, and optimizes video thumbnails for higher click-through rates.

- Generate multiple thumbnail concepts from a video, title, transcript, and target audience.
- Apply consistent branding, typography, colors, and platform-specific dimensions.
- Score concepts for clarity, emotional impact, and likely click-through performance.
- Run A/B tests and recommend the strongest thumbnail based on performance data.
- Export optimized assets for YouTube, social media, and content management systems.
